Bluff Game

My MSc dissertation: a turn-based card bluffing game in Unreal Engine 5, with three AI opponents — Tactical, Manipulator and Adaptive — that each read and commit to bluffs differently.

All three extend one base class and share a single decision pipeline, so they differ through tuning, not duplicated logic. Supporting systems cover turn order, hand management and player stats, with a full UI flow from declarations to the win screen.

Unreal Engine 5 Blueprints AI Design

3D Chase Game

MSc coursework for Advanced Game Technologies in C++ and OpenGL on Newcastle's nclgl framework. Roll a physics-driven ball through a maze as AI hunters close in — detouring for power-ups when it pays.

Each hunter runs a behaviour tree: a selector tries a find-and-path-to-power-up sequence, falling back to a direct chase. Movement is A* over a navigation grid, re-pathed on a timer to track the player. Plus OBB wall collision, respawning pickups and a menu, play and game-over state machine.

C++ OpenGL Behaviour Trees Pathfinding

Volcano Eruption

MSc coursework for Advanced Graphics for Games in C++ and OpenGL on Newcastle's nclgl framework. A multi-pass volcanic scene over shadow-mapped terrain, with a 1,200-particle eruption of lava, vapour and smoke.

Passes: a 2048² depth-only shadow map, skybox, heightmap terrain and a frustum-culled scene graph. The terrain shader blends textures by altitude, layers three noise scales to hide tiling, and adds bump mapping and contact shadows. Each particle type has its own gravity, lifetime and blend mode; day/night, eruption intensity and ground cracking toggle at runtime.

C++ OpenGL GLSL

Push/Pull Parkour

A six-person MSc team project: a push/pull parkour game in C++ for PC and PS5, built through Jenkins CI. I owned most of the UI, the animation pipeline and the input layer, plus some gameplay.

The animation pipeline imports GLTF and Assimp models into an ozz-animation runtime with GPU skinning. The UI state machine covers menus, level select, controls, pause, end-of-level, and local and networked play. I also mapped keyboard, mouse and PS5 DualSense input, and worked on player movement and multiplayer fixes.

C++ OpenGL PS5 Skeletal Animation Team Project

Air Hockey — Neon Arcade

A single-player neon air hockey game in React, TypeScript and Vite, running on my own Canvas game engine — fixed-timestep physics, collision detection, predictive AI and touch controls.

Physics substeps to stop a fast puck tunnelling, handling momentum transfer, friction and corner cases. A state-machine AI — defend, track, attack, return, clear — predicts the puck's path including wall bounces, across several difficulty profiles. Plus Web Audio sound, goal-only camera shake, full mobile support and 21 automated edge-case tests.

TypeScript React HTML5 Canvas Custom Engine

Sprite Flight

A 2D score-attack survival game in Unity. Steer a craft through a field of obstacles, each spawned at a random scale and impulse, so no two runs play the same.

Score climbs with survival time; a collision triggers a particle burst and an instant restart. Built on Unity's Input System and UI Toolkit rather than the legacy stacks, with Rigidbody2D movement in FixedUpdate for framerate-independent handling.
